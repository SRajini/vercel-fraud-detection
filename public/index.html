Insurance Claim Fraud Risk Scorer

Enter claim details to get a fraud risk score. Higher-risk claims should be prioritised for investigator review.

Demo model: trained on synthetic data so the site works out of the box. Run training/export_for_vercel.py on the real Kaggle dataset and redeploy.

Model performance (held-out test set)

Logistic Regression with class weighting. The decision threshold favours recall, because missing a fraudulent claim costs more than reviewing an extra one. Fields not shown here use typical (median or most common) values.
